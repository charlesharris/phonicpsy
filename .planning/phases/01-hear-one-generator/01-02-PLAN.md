<objective>
Turn the skeleton's hardcoded musical defaults into real, validated knobs — `--scale`, `--root`,
`--out`, and a rolled-and-printed seed — and prove that turning them cannot take the output out of
key.

Purpose: GEN-04 and GEN-06 only become meaningful when there is a *declared* scale to be locked
to. In plan 01-01 the scale was a constant, so "pitches are quantized to the declared scale" was
true the way a tautology is true. This plan makes the scale a parameter that reaches the emitted
`chord_degree` call, adds the loud allowlist validation D-11 specifies, and then proves
structurally that `chord_degree` is the *only* pitch source in the emitted program — which is what
makes GEN-06 a property of the architecture rather than a post-generation filter.

The user can now do something they could not do after plan 01-01: choose the key and the mode of
what they are about to hear, and get told immediately and clearly when they ask for a scale Sonic
Pi does not have.

Output: a CLI with the full Phase 1 knob surface, `Theory::Note`, the complete 157-name allowlist,
and tests that fix the scale-lock property in place before Phases 2-4 build generators on top of it.

Locked decisions implemented here: D-03 (plain keyword arguments, still no param DSL), D-07 (no
`--seed` rolls a seed and always prints it), D-09 (`--scale` / `--root` are flags, explicitly
because the user asked for these to be changeable), D-10 (A minor pentatonic remains the default
when neither flag is passed), D-11 (`--scale` accepts any scale name Sonic Pi knows, validated in
the theory core, failing loudly at the CLI), D-19 (`out/<generator>-<seed>.rb` default,
overridable with `--out`).
</objective>
