## Flagged Assumption Worth Your Attention

**GEN-04 interpretation.** The emitted code calls Sonic Pi's `chord_degree` rather than a bare
`scale` call. That is what makes GEN-06 *structural* — `Chord.resolve_degree` builds voicings out
of the named scale itself, so an out-of-scale pitch is impossible by construction rather than
filtered after the fact. A strict reading of GEN-04's "`scale`/`chord`" wording could expect a
literal `scale` call. If that reading matters to you, raise it before executing wave 2.
